How to use the recorder

    
    
      
  1. Connect the phone with USB debugging on and press Refresh. Setup check lists anything missing.
    2. 
      
  2. Record: click on the phone picture to tap; drag to swipe. Pick another action (Type, Assert, Wait for...) in the toolbar first. Each step suggests checks: tick the ones the test should prove.
    3. 
      
  3. Save & run: give the test a name, choose Plain or POM, press Save, then Run. Results, screenshots and the likely cause of a failure are in Reports.
